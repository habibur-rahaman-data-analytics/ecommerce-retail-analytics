# E-commerce & Retail Analytics

**An end-to-end data analytics project to investigate sales performance, delivery delays, customer satisfaction, and logistics costs.**

---

## Project Overview

This project aims to analyze e-commerce order and sales data to identify business performance trends, investigate delivery-related issues, and understand factors associated with customer satisfaction and profitability.

The analysis follows a business-focused approach: define the problem, prepare the data, calculate KPIs, investigate patterns, visualize findings, and develop actionable recommendations.

## Business Objectives

- Identify product categories and regions generating the highest revenue.
- Evaluate revenue performance by seller and product category.
- Measure the percentage of orders delivered after the estimated delivery date.
- Investigate the relationship between delivery delays and customer review scores.
- Identify product categories with high freight costs or poor customer satisfaction.

## Business Questions

### 1. Sales and Revenue Performance
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which regions contribute most to total sales?
- How does revenue change over time?

### 2. Seller and Product Performance
- How much revenue is generated by each seller and product category?
- Which sellers and categories perform strongly or poorly?
- Which products may require further performance investigation?

### 3. Delivery Performance
- What percentage of orders arrive after their estimated delivery date?
- Which regions or product categories experience more delays?
- How does actual delivery time compare with estimated delivery time?

### 4. Customer Satisfaction
- Are late deliveries associated with lower customer review scores?
- Which product categories have lower average review scores?
- Which delivery or product patterns deserve further investigation?

### 5. Freight Cost Analysis
- Which product categories have higher freight costs?
- How do freight costs vary by region and product category?
- Where could logistics costs be investigated for potential improvement?

## Project Workflow

1. **Business Understanding** — Define the business questions and analysis objectives.
2. **Data Understanding** — Review dataset structure, columns, data types, and relationships.
3. **Data Cleaning** — Check missing values, duplicates, inconsistent formats, and data quality.
4. **Data Preparation** — Prepare order, product, seller, delivery, review, and freight-related fields as needed.
5. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** — Investigate distributions, trends, and relationships.
6. **KPI Analysis** — Calculate revenue, late-delivery rate, review-score metrics, and freight-cost measures.
7. **Root Cause Investigation** — Compare performance across relevant categories, regions, and time periods.
8. **Dashboard Development** — Present important metrics and patterns in Power BI.
9. **Recommendations** — Propose practical actions supported by the analysis.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs are planned for the project. Final definitions should match the selected dataset and business requirements.

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Sum of the selected sales or order-value measure |
| Revenue by Category | Revenue grouped by product category |
| Revenue by Seller | Revenue grouped by seller |
| Late Delivery Rate | Eligible delivered orders arriving after the estimated delivery date divided by eligible delivered orders |
| Average Review Score | Average of valid customer review scores |
| Average Freight Cost | Average freight cost per defined unit, such as order or item |
| Delivery Time | Time between the defined order and delivery timestamps |

**Important:** Define the denominator for each KPI clearly. Exclude cancelled orders and orders without sufficient delivery information where appropriate, and document the rules used.
